The icons in godot_icons.tsv (one 'Name<TAB>SVG' per line) are Godot's editor icons,
taken from editor/icons and modules/*/icons (line breaks and tabs turned into spaces) of Godot 4.7.1-stable (commit a13da4feb8d8aefc283c3763d33a2f170a18d541):
https://github.com/godotengine/godot/tree/4.7.1-stable/editor/icons
https://github.com/godotengine/godot/tree/4.7.1-stable/modules (each module's icons folder)
